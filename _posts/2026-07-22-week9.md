I developed an ablation test idea and discussed it with my advisors. The proposed structure adds modalities one at a time: text and gesture, voice and gesture, clarification, gaze, robot repositioning, full movement, and an exocentric human-view camera.

The ablation design was a way to make the project measurable. Instead of building one large system and hoping it worked, each condition tests a specific contribution: whether voice changes interaction compared with typed text, whether clarifying dialogue helps when confidence is low, whether gaze improves interpretation, and whether movement gives Spot a better view.

I also began implementing ideas from prior papers using [MediaPipe Pose Landmarker](https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/pose_landmarker) for pointing and pose estimation. The goal was to estimate the shoulder-to-wrist pointing ray and compare it with candidate object locations. This gave the system a way to report not just a final answer, but the gesture angle and confidence behind that answer. This first version is archived as [ablation_MEDIAPIPE](https://github.com/juleechung/multimodal_hri/tree/main/ablation_MEDIAPIPE).
