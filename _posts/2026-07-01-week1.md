This week I began shaping the DREAM project around multimodal human-robot interaction with Boston Dynamics' Spot robot. The initial focus is to clarify the interaction problem: how a robot should understand and respond to human communication that may include speech, gesture, spatial context, and social cues.

I am also mapping the project toward a possible museum application. Museums are rich public environments where visitors, staff, objects, architecture, and institutional norms all affect how interaction unfolds. That makes them a compelling test case for socially aware robots: a robot must be useful without being intrusive, legible without being simplistic, and responsive to context rather than only commands.

My immediate goals are to review related HRI work, define the project scope with my advisors, organize the `gesturehri` repository, and identify a first prototype that can connect perception, gesture interpretation, and robot behavior.
