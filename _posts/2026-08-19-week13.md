I began moving from the earlier MediaPipe-based prototype toward a more robust perception stack. The main direction was to test YOLO for person/pose detection and object boxes, while keeping the ablation design intact.

The motivation was practical. MediaPipe worked for close, clean views, but it struggled when the person was small, far away, or only partly visible in Spot's cameras. Because pointing is central to the project, missed or unstable body keypoints can make the entire grounding pipeline fragile. I started looking at YOLOv8 pose as a more reliable way to detect a person, estimate skeleton keypoints, and draw the pointing ray across live camera feeds.

This week also clarified the difference between building a demo and building a study. A demo only needs one successful run; a study needs the same pipeline to behave consistently across conditions. That pushed the code toward shared utilities, clearer task boundaries, and logging that can support later evaluation.
